description: Tells the model how to summarise a conversation so the assistant can continue without the full text.
tasks:
  - summary
when: When a conversation's context fills up, and on /compact.
---
Summarise this conversation between a user and an AI development assistant, so the assistant can continue the work without the full text. Keep: the goal and the user's requests, decisions and their reasons, constraints and preferences the user stated, files and commands that matter, what was done and what is still open, and errors with their causes. Skills and standards stay available apart from the summary, so do not summarise their text. Leave out pleasantries and repeated details. Write in the language of the conversation, as compact notes under short headings.
